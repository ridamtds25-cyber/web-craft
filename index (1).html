7. Business Improvement Suggestions

  1. Post every new mobile launch on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp.
  2. Create short product-feature videos and comparison posts.
  3. Keep the Google Business Profile updated.
  4. Collect genuine customer reviews after purchase.
  5. Promote exchange offers and festival discounts digitally.
  6. Track which posts and offers get the best response.
